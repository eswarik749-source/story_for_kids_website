The Tortoise and the Hare

Once upon a time, a proud hare lived in a beautiful forest. He always laughed at the slow-moving tortoise. One day, the tortoise challenged the hare to a race. The hare ran very fast and soon went far ahead. Feeling confident, the hare decided to take a nap under a tree. Meanwhile, the tortoise continued walking slowly without stopping. Finally, the tortoise reached the finish line first! The hare woke up and realized his mistake. Everyone celebrated the tortoise's victory.

💡 Moral of the Story: Slow and steady wins the race!
